# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Hydra Toon to be a welcoming, constructive place to collaborate.
Treat others with respect regardless of background, identity, experience or
point of view. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Give useful, specific feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect differing perspectives and accept constructive criticism.
- Be considerate of others' time and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
sharing someone's private information without consent, and repeated disruptive
behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers privately using contact information on
their GitHub profiles. If you cannot find a private channel, open an issue
requesting one **without naming people or describing the incident**. Do not
post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly,
avoid conflicts of interest where possible, and may remove content, issue a
warning, or restrict participation depending on the circumstances.
