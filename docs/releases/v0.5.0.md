# v0.5.0 — Reusable RGBA and USD interoperability

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-10-19. Tag `v0.5.0` follows a green
[release workflow dry run](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut) on the prepared
`main`. The tagged workflow creates a draft; a human publishes it.

## What this milestone establishes

The Vulkan renderer produces background-independent premultiplied linear-sRGB
RGBA8. Material opacity, silhouette coverage, toon hulls, Mask/A2C and MSAA
resolve contribute their actual alpha. Hydra preserves that product in its
colour AOV. The viewport consumes an independent foreground target, composites
solid/checkerboard previews on the GPU and exports straight sRGB RGBA PNG
without preview backgrounds or UI. Public interpretation and conversions
follow the [render output policy](../design/RENDER_OUTPUT_POLICY.md).
Renderer reports [78](../reports/renderer/78-2026-10-18-offscreen-rgba.md),
[79](../reports/renderer/79-2026-10-18-hydra-rgba.md),
[80](../reports/renderer/80-2026-10-18-viewport-compositing.md),
[81](../reports/renderer/81-2026-10-19-foreground-png.md) and
[82](../reports/renderer/82-2026-10-19-usdview-rgba-consumer.md).

Ordinary USD meshes gain textured metallic/roughness PreviewSurface,
normal maps, corner primvars and an explicit unreadable-material fallback.
Unbound meshes receive lit display colour. Authored rest tangent frames and
generated MikkTSpace frames retain mirrored seams and skin/morph data;
MToon and PreviewSurface share their deformation. The
[capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) identifies supported
inputs and fallbacks. Reports 68–77 in the [renderer index](../reports/renderer/)
record their implementation evidence.

## Bounded validation

Two original, redistributable fixtures exercise an outlined MToon avatar shape,
textured transparent hair and Mask, PreviewSurface emission and normal maps,
and MMD self-shadow on a receiver. Their 1x/4x RGBA products are inspected over
black, white, checkerboard and an arbitrary procedural image, with independent
PNG reconstruction and native opaque-background controls. An issue fixes the
tangent asset set and a 15-minute reference budget before capture; one existing
Storm comparison and the analytic authored/generated deformation checks finish
that run. The results and stop decision are
[report 83](../reports/renderer/83-2026-10-19-v050-acceptance.md).

## Compatibility and artifacts

- Windows x86_64, Vulkan 1.3 and canonical CY2026 OpenUSD 26.08 `lookdev`;
  measured configuration in [supported configurations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).
- Transparent MSAA requires Vulkan `alphaToOne`. Unsupported requests fail
  explicitly; transparent 1x remains available.
- Hydra colour AOV: bottom-row-first, linear sRGB, linear transfer,
  premultiplied RGBA8. Convert explicitly for a host requiring another
  convention. Viewport foreground/PNG origin is top-left; PNG is straight
  sRGB RGBA with encoding metadata.
- `toon:backgroundMode` and `toon:backgroundColor` select the scene clear;
  viewport preview backgrounds are independent. `--foreground-output FILE.png`
  with `--frames N` exports the final foreground.
- The release lane produces the `hydra` binary package, manifest, SPDX SBOM,
  source archive, checksums and archive/OCI pins. No published digest is
  claimed before the tagged workflow. The published package still needs the
  Formation GPU check described in the [release procedure](README.md).

## Known limitations

- Native usdview transparent sRGB display/export has an alpha/encoding
  boundary defect. It is unsupported for this release. The controlled
  uncorrected-linear-framebuffer conversion is test evidence, not a native
  host repair or end-user command; use viewport foreground PNG or an explicit
  AOV consumer conversion. Opaque host displays agree with that conversion.
- The bounded full-material fixture is renderer-core acceptance, not a full
  textured-avatar Hydra-AOV or arbitrary-host certification. Image backgrounds
  in that acceptance are an external CPU consumer; the viewport offers solid
  and checkerboard backgrounds.
- Linear RGBA8 limits precision at low alpha. Non-sRGB swapchains, devices
  lacking `alphaToOne` and non-NVIDIA GPUs remain unverified by these runs.
- External authored-asset/deformed-frame and glTF-engine tangent equivalence,
  tangent morph deltas and CPU-deformed generated frames are not established.
  Further reference work requires a concrete visible defect, frame semantic
  change or recurrence under the material stop rule.
- Known intermediate-metalness and transformed-normal differences from Storm
  are accepted, with independent controls in reports 75–76. Pixel-identical
  Storm output, arbitrary graphs, specular workflow, IBL, MaterialX/glTF PBR,
  shared cross-material shadows, WebGPU and GPU handoff are outside this
  milestone. MMD retains the limits recorded in [v0.4.0](v0.4.0.md#known-limitations).
