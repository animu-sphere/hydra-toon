# v0.7.0 — WebGPU

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-10-25. Tag `v0.7.0` follows a green
[release workflow dry run](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut) on the prepared
`main`. The tagged workflow creates a draft; a human publishes it.

## What this milestone establishes

A second backend, `backend/webgpu/`, reproduces the Vulkan contracts
through v0.6.0 from the same core extraction, under the
[WebGPU policy](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md) and the
[backend boundary](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#4-backends). It is optional
and native Windows only; `hdToon` and the published package stay on Vulkan.

- **Dawn, pinned.** `Toon::WebGPU` is written against `webgpu.h` on a
  pinned Dawn release, its Direct3D 12 adapter by default and its Vulkan
  adapter for diagnosis, with the pinned DXC's `dxcompiler.dll` beside the
  host. `ost build --intent webgpu` (`TOON_ENABLE_WEBGPU`) builds it with
  the headless runner and viewport; `--intent webgpu-usd` adds the Hydra
  adapter so `toon-viewport --backend webgpu --usd <stage>` draws USD
  stages through Dawn.
- **One shader source.** The Slang shaders move to the root `shaders/`,
  grouped by role, behind one bindings module per backend
  ([WGPU-Q2](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#11-open-questions), resolved), and
  generate WGSL that the pinned Tint validates in the build. The Vulkan
  backend's SPIR-V is unchanged.
- **Shared contract code.** Draw constants, frame rows, parameter records,
  shadow view records, flags and their writers move to `backend/shared/`
  (`Toon::BackendShared`), and the backend-neutral public types to
  `include/toon/backend.hpp`; existing consumers compile unchanged.
- **The renderer model on WebGPU.** Unlit, PreviewSurface, MToon and MMD
  pipelines on per-material bind groups; outlines and their culling; GPU
  skinning, morphs and the late latch; 4x with MToon Mask's coverage
  through the sample mask; the shared shadow views and every family's
  receivers; both background modes and the reusable RGBA products; a
  present session with the composite, captures, foreground export, overlay
  and pass-level GPU timing.

Stages 1 to 3 are renderer reports
[91](../reports/renderer/91-2026-10-24-webgpu-stage-1.md),
[92](../reports/renderer/92-2026-10-25-webgpu-stage-2.md) and
[93](../reports/renderer/93-2026-10-25-webgpu-stage-3.md); the toolchain
probe behind the policy is
[report 90](../reports/renderer/90-2026-10-24-webgpu-toolchain-probe.md).
The [capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) owns current
status.

## Acceptance

[Renderer report 94](../reports/renderer/94-2026-10-25-webgpu-acceptance.md)
meets the milestone's done-when condition on one machine, within
[WGPU-Q1](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#11-open-questions)'s limits, without
pixel identity:

- Offscreen, `toon-webgpu-parity`'s twenty-two canonical scenes, six with
  shadows, hold the limits at 1x and 4x over both backgrounds, drawing the
  same shadow views and casters; the RGBA output, morph and late-latch GPU
  checks and the 31 headless checks pass on WebGPU.
- In the viewport every committed USD stage, twenty, is within one linear
  step of Vulkan at 1x and 4x over both backgrounds, with equal scene,
  upload, draw, hull and shadow-view statistics; six are CTests
  (`toon-viewport-webgpu-usd-parity-*`).
- Local AliciaSolid, YYB Miku and a kitchen holding both differ only on
  mesh and hull edges through Direct3D 12, and are byte-identical through
  Dawn's Vulkan adapter.
- The `shadow-cost` fixtures record p95/p99 on both backends
  (`toon-viewport-webgpu-shadow-cost-1`, `-4`), their GPU passes within
  0.05 ms of each other; the avatars' figures are recorded beside them.

The release gate's local runs are
[report 95](../reports/renderer/95-2026-10-25-v070-preparation.md).

## Compatibility and artifacts

- Windows x86_64, Vulkan 1.3 and canonical CY2026 OpenUSD 26.08 `lookdev`;
  the WebGPU backend needs a Direct3D 12 (or Vulkan) adapter through Dawn.
  Measured configuration in
  [supported configurations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).
- The `core`, `hydra` and `viewport-usd` intents and the published `hydra`
  package do not change in content: no Dawn, DXC or WGSL file enters them,
  and the Vulkan backend's output is unchanged. The WebGPU backend is built
  from source with the `webgpu` or `webgpu-usd` intent.
- The shaders' source path moves from `backend/vulkan/shaders/` to
  `shaders/`; compiled artifacts keep their names. Backend-neutral types
  now live in `include/toon/backend.hpp`, which the Vulkan headers include.
- The release lane produces the `hydra` binary package, manifest, SPDX SBOM,
  source archive, checksums and archive/OCI pins. No published digest is
  claimed before the tagged workflow. The published package still needs the
  Formation GPU check described in the [release procedure](README.md).

## Known limitations

- Native Windows only, on one machine and GPU, NVIDIA RTX A5000. Browser,
  WASM, a Hydra or Hgi WebGPU path, GPU texture handoff and Linux are
  outside the policy
  ([WebGPU policy §12](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#12-outside-this-policy)).
- The WebGPU session times whole passes only, core WebGPU's timestamps;
  the scene's parts and the image wait are unmeasured.
- Dense avatars' edge differences through Direct3D 12 are recorded, not
  CTest limits, as the avatars cannot be committed.
- The Vulkan backend keeps vertex, skin and morph buffers in host-visible
  memory, which costs a dense avatar about 0.4 ms of GPU frame on a
  discrete GPU
  ([report 94 §4](../reports/renderer/94-2026-10-25-webgpu-acceptance.md#4-performance));
  it waits in the [later roadmap](../roadmap/later.md#not-yet-in-a-milestone).
- Material, shadow, output and host limits are those of
  [v0.6.0](v0.6.0.md#known-limitations) and the releases it names.
