# v0.6.0 — Shared shadows

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-10-21. Tag `v0.6.0` follows a green
[release workflow dry run](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut) on the prepared
`main`. The tagged workflow creates a draft; a human publishes it.

## What this milestone establishes

MToon, MMD, PreviewSurface and unbound meshes cast onto and receive from each
other in one scene, under the [shadow policy](../design/SHADOW_POLICY.md),
each family keeping its own shading
([material policy §14](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#14-shadows-across-material-families)).

- **One map per shadowing light, drawn by one shared caster pass**
  ([MAT-Q7](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#9-open-questions), resolved). The
  core's `ShadowFit` fits up to four views: directional lights orthographic,
  spot lights (cones up to 80 degrees) perspective, or the camera key when
  the scene has no shadowing light. They are the layers of one D32 array
  image; further lights are counted. Casters draw at their skinned or
  morphed positions, Mask cut, and a pose or weight update uploads nothing
  static.
- **MMD's self-shadow is the shared view of the first direct light.** Its
  receivers keep [material policy §10](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#10-mmds-self-shadow)'s
  comparison and filter. An MMD-only scene draws byte-for-byte as in
  v0.5.0; other families' casters now shadow MMD receivers while shared
  shadows are on.
- **MToon and PreviewSurface receive.** MToon scales a light's ramped
  shading term by its visibility and leaves the rim's light unshadowed, as
  three-vrm does; PreviewSurface scales the direct response.
- **Settings and diagnostics.** `toon:shadows`/`--shadows` (on by default)
  and UsdLux `shadow:enable`; `toon:shadowResolution`,
  `toon:shadowDepthBias`, `toon:shadowNormalBias` and
  `toon:shadowFilterRadius` with measured defaults of 2,048, 1.5, 1.0 and
  1.5 texels; a shadow material view, the viewport's Shadow views panel,
  and statistics, Hydra evidence and telemetry of views, caster draws,
  memory and the fit's CPU time.

Stages 1 and 2 are renderer reports
[85](../reports/renderer/85-2026-10-21-shared-shadow-stage-1.md) and
[86](../reports/renderer/86-2026-10-21-shared-shadow-stage-2.md); the
[capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) owns current status.

## Acceptance

[Renderer report 87](../reports/renderer/87-2026-10-21-shared-shadow-acceptance.md)
meets the milestone's done-when condition on one machine:

- `renderer.shadow.cross_family` draws every receiving family under every
  casting one, static, skinned and morphed, with shared shadows and MMD's
  self-shadow on and off: 192 cases as the policy says, and each family
  shadowing itself.
- Through the runtime's providers the Hydra and fast paths draw the same
  shadow views with the same casters and identical images.
- MMD's eight reference frames of 布伦妮 are byte-identical to v0.5.0's.
  MToon's shadows-off frames are byte-identical to v0.5.0's; its shadows-on
  frames of AliciaSolid are the new reference, by digest.
- The original `shadow-cost.usda` and `shadow-cost-4.usda` fixtures
  (`toon-viewport-shadow-cost-1`, `-4`) record the GPU shadow pass, GPU
  work, CPU fit and submit with mean, p95 and p99, and the views' memory:
  four 2,048-texel views of sixteen moving casters take about 0.14 ms of
  GPU and 64 MiB.

The release gate's local runs are
[report 88](../reports/renderer/88-2026-10-21-v060-preparation.md).

## Compatibility and artifacts

- Windows x86_64, Vulkan 1.3 and canonical CY2026 OpenUSD 26.08 `lookdev`;
  measured configuration in [supported configurations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).
- Shared shadows are on by default, so MToon and PreviewSurface scenes with
  a casting mesh change their look; `toon:shadows` off (`--shadows off`)
  gives v0.5.0's frames back.
- The `mmd_shadow` shader and `SceneShaders::mmd_shadow_*` are renamed
  `shadow` and `SceneShaders::shadow_*`; `ShadowFit::Update` takes the
  resolution from the draw list.
- The release lane produces the `hydra` binary package, manifest, SPDX SBOM,
  source archive, checksums and archive/OCI pins. No published digest is
  claimed before the tagged workflow. The published package still needs the
  Formation GPU check described in the [release procedure](README.md).

## Known limitations

- Directional and spot lights only, at most four views. Point-light cube
  shadows, cascades, contact and advanced soft shadows, shadow catchers,
  translucent or coloured shadows, UsdLux shadow colour, distance and
  falloff, and shadow linking are outside the policy
  ([shadow policy §10](../design/SHADOW_POLICY.md#10-outside-this-policy)).
- A coarse mesh shows a shadow terminator, smooth normals over flat
  facets, which the normal bias only blurs
  ([report 86](../reports/renderer/86-2026-10-21-shared-shadow-stage-2.md#6-limits-and-next)).
- One machine and GPU, NVIDIA RTX A5000. MToon's reference is a
  non-redistributable avatar, kept by digest. Other GPUs and Linux follow
  [platform and GPU coverage](../roadmap/later.md#platform-and-gpu-coverage).
- MMD retains the limits recorded in [v0.4.0](v0.4.0.md#known-limitations),
  and output, host and reference limits are those of
  [v0.5.0](v0.5.0.md#known-limitations). WebGPU and GPU handoff are outside
  this milestone.
