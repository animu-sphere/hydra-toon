# v0.4.0 — MMD realization

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-10-17. Tag `v0.4.0` follows a green
[release workflow dry run](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut) on `main`.
The tagged workflow creates a draft; a human publishes it.

## What this milestone establishes

MMD's material and toon look is a first-class capability, drawn from the
canonical USD stage `usd-mmd-plugins` authors, on the material, morph and
animation paths v0.2.0 and v0.3.0 built for MToon. Parsing PMX and MMD's
semantics stay in `usd-mmd-plugins`. Each MMD behaviour the renderer
reproduces was settled against frames the project took in MikuMikuDance
v9.32 from the same camera and light, and the open questions of
[material policy §9](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#9-open-questions),
MAT-Q1 to MAT-Q6, are resolved.

- **Reading MMD materials.** `hdToon` selects MMD from `mmdImaging`'s
  Hydra view, normalizes it into the MMD block of `ToonMaterial`, and draws
  a mesh once per bound `GeomSubset`; all 495 materials of 41 local MMD
  stages read as MMD (MAT-Q1). Renderer reports
  [53](../reports/renderer/53-2026-10-11-mmd-material-read.md) and
  [54](../reports/renderer/54-2026-10-11-geom-subset-bindings.md).
- **MMD's surface.** `mmd_opaque` and `mmd_transparent` shade as
  MikuMikuEffect's `full.fx` reproduces MMD, on stored colour values
  converted to linear once (MAT-Q3), with the renderer's own shared toon
  ramps generated from MMD's colours, no MMD image copied (MAT-Q2), and
  sub-texture spheres at the mesh's additional UV. Reports
  [55](../reports/renderer/55-2026-10-11-mat-q3-mmd-colour.md),
  [56](../reports/renderer/56-2026-10-11-mmd-surface.md) and
  [59](../reports/renderer/59-2026-10-12-mmd-sub-texture.md).
- **MMD's edge.** `mmd_outline` draws `edgeSize` × `edgeScale` as a share
  of the target's height that falls with depth as MMD's does, fitted to
  MMD's frames from five cameras (MAT-Q4). Reports
  [57](../reports/renderer/57-2026-10-11-mmd-outline.md) and
  [58](../reports/renderer/58-2026-10-12-mat-q4-edge-width.md).
- **MMD's self-shadow.** `mmd_shadow`, the eighth scene pipeline, draws a
  map that follows the camera as MMD's does; receivers are shaded as
  `full.fx`'s self-shadow pass, with edges as soft as MMD's and a depth
  comparison fitted to MMD's contact and grazing shadows (MAT-Q5). On by
  default, with a host switch. Reports
  [60](../reports/renderer/60-2026-10-12-mmd-self-shadow.md),
  [63](../reports/renderer/63-2026-10-17-self-shadow-quality.md) and
  [66](../reports/renderer/66-2026-10-17-mat-q5-shadow-depth.md).
- **MMD's morphs.** Evaluated material morphs reach the canonical inputs
  and change the frame as they change MMD's; their texture, sphere and
  toon tints are carried, and drawn only when a host asks for `full.fx`'s
  reading, as MMD draws none (MAT-Q6). Vertex morphs, the stage's blend
  shapes, draw through v0.3.0's GPU morphs as MMD draws them. MMD surfaces
  pass depth ties, as MMD's LESSEQUAL does, so layers on shared vertices
  draw. Reports
  [61](../reports/renderer/61-2026-10-12-mmd-material-morph.md),
  [62](../reports/renderer/62-2026-10-17-mat-q6-morph-tints.md),
  [64](../reports/renderer/64-2026-10-17-mmd-material-morph-evidence.md) and
  [65](../reports/renderer/65-2026-10-17-mmd-vertex-morph-evidence.md).

Against MMD's frames from the same cameras, without self-shadow, Sour式初音ミク
`Black`'s full body is 3.35 levels from MMD's on average, YYB Hatsune Miku's
face close-up 3.49 and Sour's 1.89; with self-shadow, 布伦妮's full body
4.97 and its face 2.09
([reports 64](../reports/renderer/64-2026-10-17-mmd-material-morph-evidence.md),
[65](../reports/renderer/65-2026-10-17-mmd-vertex-morph-evidence.md) and
[66](../reports/renderer/66-2026-10-17-mat-q5-shadow-depth.md)).

## Compatibility and artifacts

**Platform.** Windows x86_64, a Vulkan 1.3 device and OpenStrata's canonical
CY2026 OpenUSD 26.08 `lookdev` runtime, as in v0.3.0. Measured
configurations are in
[SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).

**MMD stages.** MMD material imaging needs `usd-mmd-plugins`' `mmdImaging`
(0.2.0, carrying `mmdSchema`) in the session. `usd-mmd-plugins` v0.2.0
publishes it only at the `usd` profile; the evidence here used a
`lookdev`-profile package built locally from that release. Without it an
MMD stage's materials draw as PreviewSurface. VRM imaging is unchanged
(`vrmImaging` 0.10.0).

**Interfaces.** The scene pipelines are eight: `mmd_shadow` joins the seven.
New host switches: `DrawList::mmd_self_shadow`, the render setting
`toon:mmdSelfShadow` and the viewport's `--mmd-self-shadow`;
`DrawList::mmd_morph_tints`, `toon:mmdMorphTints` and `--mmd-morph-tints`.
The project's version is read from `VERSION`.

**Artifacts.** The release lane produces the `hydra` renderer package with
its manifest and SPDX SBOM, a source archive, checksums and archive/OCI
pins. It pushes
`ghcr.io/animu-sphere/hydra-toon:toon-0.4.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev`.
No artifact or published digest is claimed before the tagged workflow; the
release gate's local runs are
[renderer report 67](../reports/renderer/67-2026-10-17-v040-preparation.md).
A Formation is re-pinned and GPU-tested after publication.

## Known limitations

- **Blending in stored values.** Translucent MMD materials blend in the
  linear target, where MMD blends stored values, so a dark translucent
  layer such as a blush is paler than MMD's
  ([report 65](../reports/renderer/65-2026-10-17-mmd-vertex-morph-evidence.md)).
- **Not drawn.** `groundShadow`, which needs a floor the stage does not
  have; PMX 2.1's `vertexColor`, `drawPoints` and `drawLines`, read and not
  drawn ([material policy §7](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#7-pipelines-and-parameter-buffers)).
- **MMD's settings.** The self-shadow is fitted to mode 1 at MMD's default
  self-shadow distance; neither the distance nor mode 2 is read. MMD's
  morphs are evaluated by a runtime, not the renderer: group, bone and UV
  morphs, and morphs in motion, were not compared with MMD's frames.
- **Coverage.** Three local models (YYB Hatsune Miku, Sour式初音ミク, 布伦妮)
  compared with MMD v9.32 at rest pose, physics off; no MikuMikuEffect
  frame was compared, and sub-texture spheres, which no local model uses,
  were not compared with MMD. The models' terms forbid redistribution, so the
  frames are not in the repository.
- **Unchanged from v0.3.0.** Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are
  unmeasured; generic PreviewSurface and WebGPU are later milestones.
