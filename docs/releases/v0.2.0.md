# v0.2.0 — MToon quality and the viewport foundation

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-10-04. Tag `v0.2.0` follows a green
[release workflow dry run](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut) on `main`.
The tagged workflow creates a draft; a human publishes it.

## What this milestone establishes

Representative VRM avatars draw at practical MToon quality on Windows in
the dedicated viewport and usdview. The remaining material inputs,
transparency, scene lights, outline correctness/cost work and evaluation
host are complete for this milestone. The accepted AA baseline is 4x MSAA
with residual subpixel variation documented below.

- All nine MToon texture roles, rim/MatCap, normal mapping and timed UV
  scroll/rotation; Opaque, Mask and Blend, including queue ordering and
  transparent depth writes. Evidence: renderer reports
  [14](../reports/renderer/14-2026-09-28-mtoon-rim.md),
  [15](../reports/renderer/15-2026-09-28-mtoon-transparent.md),
  [25](../reports/renderer/25-2026-09-30-mtoon-rest.md).
- Basic authored directional, point, spot and uniform ambient lights,
  with material/light diagnostics. usdview application helper lights no
  longer overexpose the avatar or suppress the camera fallback. Evidence:
  [26](../reports/renderer/26-2026-09-30-scene-lights.md),
  [32](../reports/renderer/32-2026-10-04-vrm-reproduction.md).
- Authored normals, stage-unit world widths, hull depth bias and width-map
  sampling, plus conservative zero-width, six-plane frustum and bounded
  opaque-triangle occlusion omission. Pixel/depth comparisons preserve
  uncertain hulls. Evidence: reports
  [24](../reports/renderer/24-2026-09-30-outline-stability-cost.md),
  [28](../reports/renderer/28-2026-10-04-outline-frustum.md),
  [29](../reports/renderer/29-2026-10-04-outline-depth-clip.md),
  [30](../reports/renderer/30-2026-10-04-outline-occlusion.md).
- A Hydra-fed Vulkan viewport with orbit/pan/dolly framing, runtime MSAA
  switching, file opening/replacement, timing and upload telemetry, light
  and material controls, deterministic USD time stepping, overlay-free
  screenshots, every-frame sequences and exported camera matrices.
- A measured 4x AA default: controlled finite-supersampling comparisons,
  continuous repeated VRM motion, and matched VRM 0.x/1.0 rest-pose
  comparisons against three-vrm 3.5.5 and usdview. Evidence: reports
  [27](../reports/renderer/27-2026-10-01-antialiasing-quality.md),
  [31](../reports/renderer/31-2026-10-04-outline-temporal.md),
  [32](../reports/renderer/32-2026-10-04-vrm-reproduction.md).

## Compatibility and artifacts

Windows x86_64, a Vulkan 1.3 device and OpenStrata's canonical CY2026
OpenUSD 26.08 `lookdev` runtime. VRM material imaging needs `vrmImaging`
0.10.0; raw VRM ingestion also needs matching file-format and package-resolver
plugins. `ost` 0.23.14 supports the composed renderer viewport workflow.
Measured configurations are in
[SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).

The release lane produces the `hydra` renderer package, manifest, SPDX SBOM,
source archive, checksums, and archive/OCI pins. It pushes
`ghcr.io/animu-sphere/hydra-toon:toon-0.2.0-cy2026-windows-x86_64-py313-lookdev`.
No artifact or published digest is claimed before the tagged workflow.
A Formation is re-pinned and GPU-tested after publication, as required by
the [release procedure](README.md#how-a-release-is-cut).

## Known limitations

- 4x MSAA reduces outline aliasing but retains subpixel temporal variation;
  there is no temporal filter or promise of flicker-free thin features.
  Pixel shading, texture/alpha sampling and finite reference differences
  remain. Coverage is two rest-pose assets and two motions, not every VRM.
- The VRM 1.0 comparison does not validate constraints, springs or expression
  playback. Its importer warns about conflicting inverse bind matrices.
- CPU blend shapes and dual quaternion skinning still upload points.
  Animation fast paths, late motion latching and latency telemetry belong
  to [v0.3.0](../roadmap/v0.3.0.md).
- Basic lights have no shadows, environment textures, area-light power
  normalization or host helper-light support; at most 32 direct lights.
- General avatar self-occlusion is not culled. Transparent ordering within
  a queue is draw order, and tangent/MikkTSpace equivalence is unestablished.
- The texture table remains fixed at 128 entries. Asset uploads stay on the
  render thread, Hydra reads back every frame, and picking, instancing and
  framing data-window support remain absent.
- Linux, macOS and AMD/Intel GPUs are unmeasured; generic PreviewSurface,
  MMD and WebGPU are later milestones.
