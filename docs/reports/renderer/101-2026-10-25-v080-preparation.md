evidence on this machine is reports 97 to 100:

- The reference consumer's byte-exact comparisons with the readback
  composite over both RGBA fixtures at 1x and 4x, through resize, held
  slots, several consumers, capture, rings of two to four slots and a
  recreated device, under validation and synchronization validation
  ([report 100 §2](100-2026-10-25-handoff-acceptance.md#2-the-reference-consumer)).

  kitchen holding AliciaSolid and YYB Miku, presented by the Tessera host

  ([report 100 §4](100-2026-10-25-handoff-acceptance.md#4-the-tessera-hosts-frames)).

  the Tessera host's frame on both paths
