  `mmd_opaque` and `mmd_transparent` ([report 56](56-2026-10-11-mmd-surface.md)).

## TL;DR

**An MMD material that draws its edge now adds a hull through
`mmd_outline`, the seventh scene pipeline: each skinned vertex moves out
along its normal by the material's `edgeSize` times the mesh's
`primvars:mmd:edgeScale`, front faces culled, in the edge colour, unlit and
decoded once, after every surface. MMD's edge is neither a constant length
nor a constant number of pixels: on its own frames, moving the camera from
45 to 9 MMD units makes an `edgeSize` 0.4 edge 2.6 times as wide in
pixels, not 1 or 5 times. The renderer's width is a length in MMD units
plus a width in pixels, fitted to those two distances; hdToon's edge is
1.61–1.63 pixels where MMD's is 1.55–1.59 at distance 9, and within the
measurement's reach of MMD's at 45. At distance 9 the edges bring every
compared region closer to MMD's capture. The fit rests on two distances
and one field of view, so the width model is an open question.**

## 1. MMD's edge width, measured

The reference model and conventions are [report 55](55-2026-10-11-mat-q3-mmd-colour.md)'s
and [report 56](56-2026-10-11-mmd-surface.md)'s: the Sour-style Hatsune
Miku Ver.1.02, `Black.pmx`, whose terms forbid committing it, its textures
or any render of it. Its 20 materials have edge sizes of 0.3, 0.4 and 1,
and 9 of them draw their edge; every vertex's edge scale is 1. The face
(`face`, material 0) and the body (`base`, material 5) have an edge of size
0.4 in (147, 79, 141).

Besides capture C (light 255, centre (0.80, 10, 0), distance 45), the user
took one more in MMD, at centre (0, 18, 0), distance 9, field of view 30°,
1920 × 1080, PNG with coverage in alpha: a close-up of the face.

An edge thinner than a pixel cannot be measured by its colour alone. Along
a contour, each pixel is unmixed into the edge colour and the two colours
either side of the line, and the edge's shares are summed across the line
and turned perpendicular to it by the contour's slope:

| Capture | Distance | Contour | Width (pixels) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C | 45 | the chin over the neck, left and right | 0.66, 0.65 |
| C | 45 | the top of the left arm over the background | 0.48 |
| close-up | 9 | the chin over the neck, left and right | 1.59, 1.55 |

A constant width in pixels would be the same at both distances, and a
constant length 5 times as wide at 9. The edge is 2.6 times as wide. Its
colour does not change with the light: the 71 pixels of C within 12 levels
of one of the model's edge colours (147, 79, 141) and (35, 88, 148) are
within 3 levels of it in D, taken at light 77, where the skin falls by 16%.

## 2. The width the renderer draws

`mmd_outline` moves a vertex out along its view-space normal, as
`mtoon_outline` does, by

```text
edgeSize × edgeScale × (0.0135 MMD units + 0.89 pixels)
```

a length in MMD units, which are 0.08 m on the stage
([`usd-mmd-plugins` stage contract §6](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-mmd-plugins/blob/main/docs/design/STAGE_CONTRACT.md)),
divided into the scene's unit by its `metersPerUnit`, plus a width in pixels
of the target, turned into a length at the vertex's depth as a
screen-coordinates MToon outline is. The two constants are the line through
the two distances of §1, taking 0.6 pixels at 45 and 1.57 at 9 for an
`edgeSize` of 0.4. Two points fix two constants and test nothing: whether
MMD's width is this sum, which field of view and output size it depends on,
and how, is not known yet
([material policy §9](../../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#9-open-questions)).

## 3. The change

- **Core.** `MeshSnapshot::edge_scales`, set by
  `RenderWorld::SetMeshEdgeScales` and with its own revision: MMD's
  per-vertex edge scale, empty for a mesh without one, which then scales by
  1. `HasOutline` is true for an MMD material that draws its edge with a
  finite size above zero. Setting the UVs a mesh already has now changes
  nothing, as normals and edge scales do: Hydra reports every primvar but
  points and normals through one dirty bit, so an edge scale edit re-read
  the UVs and gave them a new revision.
- **Hydra.** `HdToonMesh` reads `primvars:mmd:edgeScale` with vertex or
  varying interpolation. `TOON_HYDRA_EVIDENCE` and the viewport's scene
  summary add `draws_mmd_outline`, and the Draws panel shows it.
- **Pipelines.** Seven scene pipelines. `mmd_outline` is `mtoon_outline`'s
  hull, blended and depth-biased alike, with the edge scale as a fourth
  vertex stream, bound with stride 0 to a 1 when the mesh has none. Its
  hulls draw after every surface, each mesh's in MMD's material order, as
  MMD draws a model's edge after its materials, so a surface in front of an
  edge has written its depth and hides it. Its fragment returns the edge
  colour, decoded from sRGB to linear once (MAT-Q3), with the edge colour's
  alpha. The surface pipelines share `HullPosition` and `ScreenWidth` from
  `surface_common.slang`; the draw constant that was `units_per_meter` is
  `outline_scale`, the units per metre for `mtoon_outline` and 1 over the
  target's height for `mmd_outline`.
- **Parameter buffers.** `MmdParameters` grows to 144 bytes with the edge
  colour and size, so an edge edit rewrites one slot.

The outline omission of reports [28](28-2026-10-04-outline-frustum.md)–[30](30-2026-10-04-outline-occlusion.md)
is not applied to MMD hulls: every MMD edge is drawn.

## 4. Tests

- `renderer.material.mmd_outline` (headless, GPU), on a diamond seen
  orthographically: an edge of size 4 in a stored green of 0.5 reads 55
  beyond the surface and leaves its centre red; a light ten times dimmer
  leaves it; on a target twice as tall the same edge does not reach the
  same point; an edge scale of 0 at every vertex, or `drawEdge` off, takes
  it away; an edge colour edit rewrites one slot and uploads nothing.
- `toon-render-world`: an edge scale edit advances its own revision alone,
  setting the same scales or UVs changes nothing, and `HasOutline` for MMD.
- `toon-renderer-hydra-geom-subsets`: the mesh carries its authored edge
  scale, and an edit of it reaches the mesh without touching its points,
  topology or UVs.
- `renderer.frame.persistence` and the `usdview` smoke test count seven
  pipelines.

The OpenStrata gates passed, each with `ost validate`: `core` 15/15,
`hydra` 24/24 and `viewport-usd` 55/55; `ost package` packaged the `hydra`
build.

## 5. Against MMD's captures

The local MMD Formation, repinned to this branch's package, drew the model
in `testusdview` from MMD's camera, as [report 56 §4](56-2026-10-11-mmd-surface.md#4-against-mmds-captures)
did, at both captures' cameras:

```text
pipelines=7 draws=20 draws_mmd=20 draws_mmd_outline=9 draws_skinned=20 draws_authored_normals=20
```

Measured as §1 measures MMD's, hdToon's edge is:

| Distance | Contour | MMD (pixels) | hdToon (pixels) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 45 | the chin, left and right | 0.66, 0.65 | 0.46, 0.46 |
| 45 | the top of the left arm | 0.48 | 0.58 |
| 9 | the chin, left and right | 1.59, 1.55 | 1.61, 1.63 |

At 45 the edge is half a pixel wide and the two contours disagree in
opposite directions by 0.1–0.2 pixels, which is what this measurement
resolves there. At 9 the widths agree to 0.06 pixels.

The close-up was also drawn with report 56's package, which draws no
edges. Mean absolute difference from MMD's capture in 8-bit levels, inside
both silhouettes eroded by three pixels, with the hair, which MMD's physics
moved, left in:

| Region | Pixels | Without edges | With edges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lower face | 42,681 | 1.66 | 1.41 |
| collar | 59,491 | 10.20 | 9.85 |
| left shoulder | 39,308 | 17.61 | 17.06 |
| right shoulder | 39,378 | 8.30 | 7.80 |

At distance 45, report 56's face region goes from 3.6 to 4.0: the
half-pixel edge lands a fraction of a pixel away from MMD's along the chin
and the right cheek, and its gain at the bangs does not make that up.

## 6. What it does not do yet

- **The width model** (§2) holds at the two distances it was fitted to.
- **Hull omission** for MMD edges.
- **Edge and surface order across meshes and models.** Every MMD edge
  draws after every surface, so a transparent draw of another model in
  front of an edge does not hide it as MMD, which draws each model's edge
  after its own materials, would.
- **Sub-texture spheres, MMD's order across opacity, blending in stored
  values, shadows, vertex colour, points and lines, and morphs**, as
  [report 56 §5](56-2026-10-11-mmd-surface.md#5-what-it-does-not-do-yet)
  lists them.
