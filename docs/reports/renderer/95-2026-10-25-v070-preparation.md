evidence on this machine is reports 91 to 94:

- Vulkan/WebGPU parity offscreen on twenty-two canonical scenes at 1x and
  4x ([reports 91](91-2026-10-24-webgpu-stage-1.md)–[93](93-2026-10-25-webgpu-stage-3.md)),
  and in the viewport on every committed USD stage
  ([report 94 §2](94-2026-10-25-webgpu-acceptance.md#2-usd-parity)).
- Representative avatars: AliciaSolid, YYB Miku and a kitchen holding both,
  within one step but for edges through Direct3D 12 and byte-identical
  through Dawn's Vulkan adapter
  ([report 94 §3](94-2026-10-25-webgpu-acceptance.md#3-avatars)).
- Performance evidence: the `shadow-cost` fixtures and the avatar's mean,
  p95 and p99 on both backends
  ([report 94 §4](94-2026-10-25-webgpu-acceptance.md#4-performance)).
