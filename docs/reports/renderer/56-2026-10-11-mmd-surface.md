## TL;DR

**An MMD material now draws through `mmd_opaque`, or through
`mmd_transparent` when it can blend, with MMD's shading computed on stored
values and decoded to linear once (MAT-Q3). MMD's shared toon ramps are the
renderer's own: ten 32 × 32 ramps generated from the colours MMD's
`toon01`–`toon10` show, never copied, and MAT-Q2 is resolved. The 20
materials of the Sour-style Hatsune Miku model draw as 20 MMD draws in
`usdview`, from MMD's camera and light. Where the pose agrees with MMD's
captures, a sleeve and the thighs differ by 1 code value on average and
the face by 3 to 4, at both light colours; lowering the light from 255 to
77 scales hdToon's frame by 0.844–0.847, against MMD's 0.842. Edges are
not drawn yet.**

## 1. MAT-Q2: the shared ramps

A material whose toon source is `shared` names one of MMD's ten shared ramps
by index. They belong to MMD and are not redistributed
([material policy §9](../../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#9-open-questions)).
Their colours were read on this workstation from MMD 9.32's own files, and
the renderer generates its set from those numbers alone
(`Toon::SharedToonRamp`): no image is copied, stored or shipped.

| Index | MMD file | What it shows | The renderer's ramp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `toon01` | white rows 0–15, (205, 205, 205) rows 16–31 | the same two steps |
| 1 | `toon02` | white, then (245, 225, 225) | the same |
| 2 | `toon03` | white, then (154, 154, 154) | the same |
| 3 | `toon04` | white, then (248, 239, 235) | the same |
| 4 | `toon05` | white to row 17, grading to (255, 231, 221) by row 24 | white to row 17, linear to (255, 231, 221) at row 24 |
| 5 | `toon06` | (255, 237, 97) with a highlight band at rows 8–11, grading to (195, 172, 3) from row 21 to 25 | (255, 237, 97) to row 21, linear to (195, 172, 3) at row 25, without the highlight |
| 6–9 | `toon07`–`toon10` | white | white |

The ramps are linear textures, read as stored like every MMD texture, and
resident in texture-table entries 1–10 from the table's creation. Of the
495 materials in 41 local MMD stages, 11 use a shared ramp, all of them
index 0; 364 use their own ramp and 120 none.

## 2. The change

- **Shading** (`shaders/mmd_common.slang`), as MikuMikuEffect's `full.fx`
  reproduces MMD's: `saturate(diffuse × light + ambient)` with diffuse
  alpha, × the base texel, the sphere texel added or multiplied at the
  view-space normal (its alpha multiplying in either mode), × the toon
  ramp at `v = 0.5 − 0.5 N·L`, plus Blinn-Phong specular × the light. A
  specular power of 0 adds the specular colour everywhere, as MMD's does.
  Every term works on stored values; the result is saturated and decoded
  from sRGB to linear once, so the host's encoding writes MMD's value.
  MMD has one light: the scene's first direct light, its colour as MMD's
  light colour (0.302 is MMD's 77). Ambient lights add nothing, and a
  back face keeps its front's normal, as in MMD.
- **Textures.** `HdToonReadMaterial` now reads MMD's base, sphere and toon
  textures as linear, undecoded (MAT-Q3).
- **Pipelines.** Six scene pipelines instead of four. `mmd_opaque` draws a
  material whose diffuse alpha is 1 and whose base and sphere textures have
  no alpha below 1, with the opaque draws. Any other MMD material goes
  through `mmd_transparent`: blended, writing depth as MMD does except where
  its alpha is 0, after every opaque draw, and within a mesh in MMD's
  material order (`mmd/drawOrder`), not the order the parts are listed in.
  Whether a texture has translucent texels is found as it uploads, so the
  choice follows pixel edits as well as values. A double-sided MMD
  material draws both sides in one draw, as MMD draws it.
- **Parameter buffers.** MMD's slot is a 112-byte `MmdParameters` in its
  own storage buffer (binding 4), at the same slot number as the MToon
  buffer's, so a material is a slot in its model's buffer
  ([material policy §7](../../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#7-pipelines-and-parameter-buffers)).
  What every surface pipeline shares — the draw constants, the texture
  table, the lights and the vertex stage — moved from `mtoon_common.slang`
  into `surface_common.slang`, with the direct-light term both models use.
- **Evidence.** `TOON_HYDRA_EVIDENCE` and the viewport's scene summary add
  `draws_mmd`; the viewport's Draws panel shows it.

## 3. Tests

- `renderer.material.mmd` (headless, GPU), on hand-computed values: the
  lighting term at a light of 0.302 reads 173, MMD's 0.8416 decoded; it
  saturates at 1; lit from behind, the shared `toon01` shades to 156 and no
  ramp leaves 255; a base texel of 128 reads 55, not the 10 a decoded read
  would give; an added sphere texel of 64 reads 134, a multiplied one 4; a
  specular colour of 0.25 at power 0 reads 133; two translucent halves of
  one mesh, listed against MMD's order, blend in MMD's order and the nearer
  writes depth; an ambient edit rewrites one slot and uploads nothing.
- `toon-render-world`: the shared ramps' size, encoding and rows.
- `toon-renderer-hydra-material`: an MMD texture is read undecoded.
- `renderer.frame.persistence` and the `usdview` smoke test now count six
  pipelines.

The OpenStrata gates passed, each with `ost validate`: `core` 15/15,
`hydra` 24/24 and `viewport-usd` 55/55; `ost package` packaged the `hydra`
build.

## 4. Against MMD's captures

The model and capture set of [report 55](55-2026-10-11-mat-q3-mmd-colour.md)
(Sour-style Hatsune Miku Ver.1.02, `Black.pmx`, captures C at light 255 and
D at 77, 1920 × 1080). The model's terms forbid committing it, its textures
or any render of it, so nothing of this section is committed except the
numbers.

The local MMD Formation, repinned to this branch's package, opened the
model's stage in `testusdview` with a script that places MMD's camera and
light on the stage. The stage is MMD's model mirrored in Z at 0.08 m per
MMD unit ([`usd-mmd-plugins` stage contract §6](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-mmd-plugins/blob/main/docs/design/STAGE_CONTRACT.md)),
so MMD's camera, centre (0.80, 10, 0) at distance 45 and a vertical field of
view of 30°, is a USD camera at (0.064, 0.8, 3.6) looking down −Z; MMD's
default light direction (−0.5, −1, 0.5) is a `DistantLight` travelling
along (−0.5, −1, −0.5), its colour 1 or 0.302. With
`USDSKELIMAGING_ENABLE_NORMAL_COMPUTATIONS=1`, as the VRM sessions set it,
the skinned mesh draws the stage's authored normals:

```text
materials_mmd=20 draws=20 draws_mmd=20 draws_skinned=20 draws_authored_normals=20 pipelines=6
```

The two frames line up with MMD's: the model's silhouette sits where MMD's
does. MMD ran its physics and `usdview` draws the bind pose, so the hair,
the skirt and the tie hang differently, and those are left out of the
comparison. Mean absolute difference in 8-bit levels, inside both models'
silhouettes eroded by three pixels:

| Region | Pixels | Light 255 (C) | Light 77 (D) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| face | 3,657 | 3.6 | 3.0 |
| neck and chest | 2,500 | 4.2 | 3.7 |
| left sleeve | 847 | 1.2 | 1.2 |
| right thigh | 3,118 | 1.2 | 1.3 |
| left thigh | 1,594 | 0.9 | 1.0 |

The face's and chest's residue lies at their contours, where MMD draws its
edges and hdToon does not yet.

hdToon's own frames, measured as report 55 measured MMD's: on 128,895
pixels inside the model, between 40 and 245, the frame at 77 is the frame at
255 scaled by 0.8435 / 0.8472 / 0.8474 (R / G / B), each band of 40
levels within 0.031 of it; MMD's was 0.8421 / 0.8416 / 0.8413. Where the
remaining 0.003–0.006 comes from has not been traced.

## 5. What it does not do yet

- **Edges.** `mmd_outline`, MMD's edge with `edgeSize` × the per-vertex
  `primvars:mmd:edgeScale`, is next.
- **Sub-texture spheres.** They sample the mesh's additional UV, which no
  draw carries yet, so a `subTexture` sphere applies nothing.
- **MMD's draw order across opacity.** MMD blends every draw in material
  order; an MMD material that cannot blend draws with the opaque draws
  instead. The result is MMD's except where an earlier translucent material
  covers a later opaque one, which MMD hides behind the translucent one's
  depth and hdToon shows through it.
- **Blending in stored values.** Translucent materials blend in the linear
  target, not in MMD's stored values ([report 55 §5](55-2026-10-11-mat-q3-mmd-colour.md#5-what-it-does-not-settle)).
- **Shadows, vertex colour, points and lines.** The ground and self-shadow
  flags, the vertex-colour flag and point and line drawing are read and not
  drawn.
- **Material and vertex morphs** through v0.3.0's paths.
