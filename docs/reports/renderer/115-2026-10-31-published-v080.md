## The handoff from the published libraries

The milestone's handoff has no installed executable, and neither the
Formation nor the headless runner calls it. Its reference consumer was
therefore compiled from the tag's unmodified `validation/handoff_test.cpp`
with MSVC 14.51, in an ignored local CMake project, linked only through
`find_package(Toon 0.8.0 EXACT)` and `Toon::Vulkan` against the pulled
package, and given the package's own `bin/shaders`.

It exited zero with validation and synchronization validation on the lending
host's instance, and no message. At 1x and 4x, for the avatar and MMD shadow
fixtures, the product on a lent device equals one on an owned device byte for
byte, and handed-off frames sampled on the GPU equal the CPU composite of the
readback product exactly. Capture, several consumers, held slots, a GPU wait
for an unreached release, a resize, rings of two and four slots and a recreated
device also pass. The test refuses a device lacking a requirement. This is the
test of [report 100](100-2026-10-25-handoff-acceptance.md), now on the released
binaries; its timing, the 300-frame measurements and the Tessera host are not
rerun.

## Limits

This run verifies the published Windows package on one NVIDIA GPU. It
compiles one validation source against the package but no other consumer; the
Tessera host and `toon-handoff-measure` remain source builds, verified locally
in reports 100 and 101. The WebGPU backend is not in the published package and
is not exercised here. No new scene, asset, pose or motion coverage is added
beyond report 96. Main has moved past the tag: live-session work after v0.8.0
is outside this package and this report. Linux, macOS and other GPU vendors
remain unmeasured.
