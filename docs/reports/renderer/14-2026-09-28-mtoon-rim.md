# MToon's rim: MatCap and the parametric rim draw, and a rim edit rewrites one slot per material

- Date: 2026-09-28
- Machine: Windows 11, MSVC 14.51, NVIDIA RTX A5000
- Tooling: `ost 0.23.13`; the canonical runtime
  `openstrata-runtime-cy2026-lookdev:26.08-gl-windows-x86_64`
  (`sha256:b982656c…`) and the published `vrmImaging` 0.10.0
  (`sha256:894fd616…`) of [report 12](12-2026-09-28-published-vrmimaging.md)
- Occasion: the first Renderer Phase 2 item of
  [design policy §25](../../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#25-implementation-phases),

## TL;DR

**Both MToon pipelines add MToon 1.0's rim after emission: `matcapFactor` ×
the MatCap texture, sampled where the view-space normal points, plus the
parametric rim, both × the rim multiply texture, then mixed with the light by
`rimLightingMixFactor`. On the avatar with `vrmImaging`, 7 of the 12 MToon
materials name one MatCap image, uploaded once, so `texture_uploads` goes
from 6 to 7. A grey parametric rim on all 12 materials rewrites 12 slots and
uploads nothing; zeroing the 7 `matcapFactor`s rewrites 7.** The avatar's
MatCap is a dark sphere whose edge alone is light, so it brightens 12
pixels, at most 29 levels, at the default full-body framing.

## 1. What changed

- **Core.** `ToonMaterial`'s MToon block carries the MatCap texture and the
  rim multiply texture. Their identities are structural, like every
  texture's; the rim colour, fresnel power, lift, lighting mix and
  `matcapFactor` stay values
  ([material policy §8](../../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#8-values-that-change-at-run-time)).
- **Backend.** A material's slot grew from 224 to 352 bytes: the parametric
  rim's colour, power and lift, the lighting mix, `matcapFactor`, and each
  new texture's table entry, sampler and transform. The slot cache keeps the
  five table entries a slot was written with as one value. The MToon push
  constants now carry view-from-object's top three rows, its 3×3 and its
  translation, in place of the normal matrix, so they stay 128 bytes; a draw
  flag says the camera is orthographic.
- **Shader.** `mtoon_common.slang` derives the normal matrix from those rows
  (the cross products of the 3×3's rows over its determinant), and both
  vertex stages pass the view-space position on. `ShadeSurface` follows
  MToon 1.0's rim pseudocode:
  - V is the unit vector from the fragment to the eye, or view-space +z
    under an orthographic camera.
  - MatCap's frame is x = normalize(up × V), y = V × x, and it samples at
    (x·N, y·N) × 0.495 + 0.5 in `st`'s bottom-left convention, then through
    the texture's transform like any other texture. **Up is the camera's**,
    as three-vrm takes it; the pseudocode takes the world's. The texture
    needs no UVs, and a material without one adds no MatCap, whatever
    `matcapFactor` says, as the specification requires.
  - The parametric rim is `pow(saturate(1 − N·V + lift), max(power, ε))` ×
    its colour.
  - Their sum is multiplied by the rim multiply texture at the mesh's UVs,
    white without one, then by lerp(1, the key light + GI,
    `rimLightingMixFactor`). The specification leaves that light undefined;
    three-vrm sums the direct lights' colours and the indirect irradiance,
    and so does this, under the stand-in lights.

  `mtoon_outline` shades its hull through the same `ShadeSurface`, so the
  outline colour is mixed with the rim too, as MToon's outline lighting mix
  states.
- **`hdToon`.** The material reads `vrm/textureInfo/matcap` and
  `rimMultiply`, both as colour. MatCap's `texCoord` is not read, since it
  samples at no UVs. The adapter keeps a material's five texture roles as
  one array, in place of a field per role.

## 4. Observations

- **The avatar's MatCap is an edge light.** Its 1024×1024 image is black
  inside a circle whose edge fades to grey (mean RGB about 26, 29, 33; at most
  107). Against report 12's authored shot, the MatCap brightens 12 pixels by
  more than 24 levels, at most 29, and darkens none; zeroing
  `matcapFactor` afterwards changes no pixel by more than 16. At full-body
  framing the grazing normals that reach the circle's edge cover few pixels.
- **A grey parametric rim of power 1 is broad.** 1 − N·V is above zero
  wherever a surface is not facing the camera squarely, so 0.3 grey,
  mixed with the stand-in light, brightened 8,249 pixels by up to 103
  levels, the whole avatar lighter toward its silhouette. That is the
  authored power's shape; the avatar authors the rim black.
- **A rim edit is a value.** Twelve materials' rim colours rewrote twelve
  slots and nothing else, and seven `matcapFactor`s seven.
- **`usdview`'s camera is perspective,** so the avatar ran the per-fragment
  V; the headless check runs the orthographic one.
- **Stale objects were deleted before building.** `ninja -t deps` reported
  `#deps 0` for 6 objects in the `hydra` tree, 1 in `core` and 30 in
  `renderer-viewport`, among them the core libraries and `vulkan_scene.cpp`;
  they were deleted before the first build, so no build ran on the old
  `ToonMaterial` layout.

## 5. Not checked

Linux. The Formation without `vrmImaging`: nothing selects MToon there, so
nothing draws a rim. A camera that rolls or pitches, where the camera's up
and the world's part. A MatCap texture with a transform, or a rim multiply
texture, on a real asset: the avatar has neither a transform nor a
`rimMultiply` image. A mirrored transform, whose derived normal matrix
carries the determinant's sign. Performance: no timing was taken; the
fragment stage samples two more textures per MToon fragment.
