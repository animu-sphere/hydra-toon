## GPU evidence

The representative scenes and models are reports 53 to 66 on this machine:

- Vulkan validation was clean in every gate run.
- Three local MMD models, YYB Hatsune Miku, Sour式初音ミク and 布伦妮, were
  drawn through `mmdImaging` from MMD's camera and light and compared with
  MikuMikuDance v9.32's own frames: surfaces, edges, the self-shadow, and
  material and vertex morphs
  ([v0.4.0](../../releases/v0.4.0.md#what-this-milestone-establishes)).

  time a frame for one model, timed in the viewport
  ([report 63](63-2026-10-17-self-shadow-quality.md)).

## Limits

Formation check are not part of this report. Each needs `main` to hold
this preparation first. The MMD Formation the evidence ran in pins a
locally built `mmdImaging` at the `lookdev` profile, which
`usd-mmd-plugins` does not publish.
