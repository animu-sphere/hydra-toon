# v0.5.0 — USD interoperability and render output

Enough interoperability that an ordinary USD asset does not draw broken just
because it is not a toon material, while the renderer stays a toon renderer
and generic coverage never outranks it
([design policy §26](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#26-non-goals)); general
rendering is `hydra-merlin`'s.

The milestone establishes input and output semantics on Vulkan before WebGPU
reproduces them. Work proceeds through material interoperability, tangent
fidelity, reusable RGBA output, then compositing and reference evidence. The
contracts belong to [material policy §12–§13](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#12-previewsurface-and-generic-usd-fallback)
and [render output policy](../design/RENDER_OUTPUT_POLICY.md).

## USD interoperability

- Base colour, roughness, metallic, normal, emissive, opacity, their textures
  and basic texture transforms, in `preview_surface`: a basic PBR fallback.
- glTF metallic-roughness PBR materials mixed with MToon in a VRM avatar,
  consumed through the imported `UsdPreviewSurface` network. Preserve base
  colour and textures, metallic and roughness, normals, emission including
  its strength, and Opaque, Mask and Blend behaviour through that network.
- Seed-san reproduction checks for `backpack_metal`, `backpack_nm`,
  `backpack_plastic` and `anim_logo`, plus `wear_metal`, `green_emit` and
  `glass`: retain the backpack's texture, the logo's colour and the green
  emission, and let the glass reveal the surface behind it. Cover these
  behaviours with redistributable controlled fixtures and a local avatar
  comparison against a reference renderer; keep MToon materials unchanged.
- Generic textured meshes: face-varying `st` and the other mesh data
  ordinary assets author.
- A fallback for materials the renderer does not support.

## Tangent-space normal mapping

- Consume authored tangents, preserving handedness, and generate
  MikkTSpace-compatible tangents when they are absent.
- Share the tangent foundation between MToon and PreviewSurface normal maps;
  support mirrored UVs and stable normal/tangent frames after skinning and
  morphing, beyond the frame derived only from deformed positions and UVs.
- Compare authored-tangent assets to a reference renderer and generated
  tangents to a glTF reference, including mirrored seams and deformation.

## Reusable RGBA output

- Produce a reusable RGBA render product independent of the swapchain;
  make the viewport one consumer of it.
- Add opaque and transparent background modes under the
  [output contract](../design/RENDER_OUTPUT_POLICY.md#2-background-and-environment).
  Keep material transparency independent of background transparency.
- Establish premultiplied internal RGBA, correct surface and outline alpha,
  multisample colour/alpha resolve, Mask coverage and Blend compositing.
- Preserve RGBA through Hydra colour AOVs and readback, converting at the
  boundary if the host's convention differs.
- Composite the scene product in the dedicated viewport over a selectable
  background colour or checkerboard. A transparent native window is not a
  requirement.

GPU texture sharing and zero-copy handoff belong to the
[host/compositor milestone](later.md#host-and-compositor-integration-v070-scale),
not this milestone. Environment lighting, ground/contact/drop shadows and
shadow catchers are outside this milestone.

## Reference evidence and release condition

Use redistributable controlled fixtures and local reference comparisons;
keep restricted assets and captures out of the repository. Record Vulkan
reference images for:

| Material family | Cases |
| --- | --- |
| MToon | Opaque avatar, outline, Mask, Blend, transparent hair, emissive, normal map |
| MMD | Surface material, edge, self-shadow, transparent material |
| PreviewSurface | Opaque, Mask, Blend, normal map, emissive |

Inspect each transparent RGBA product and composite the same product over
white, black, checkerboard and an arbitrary image. Compare each against its
expected foreground-over-background result, including silhouette, outline,
Mask and MSAA edges; detect fringes, halos and alpha errors. Background
selection must not alter the foreground product.

**Done when** VRM, MMD and generic USD assets render into a reusable RGBA
image without assuming a background: basic PreviewSurface PBR and glTF
metallic-roughness fallback, texture transforms, normal/emissive/opacity,
unsupported-material fallback, authored/generated tangent fidelity,
premultiplied alpha, MSAA edge alpha, toon outlines and transparent materials
all satisfy the reference checks, with Hydra alpha preservation and viewport
background compositing.
