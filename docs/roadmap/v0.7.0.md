# v0.7.0 — WebGPU

Unfinished work of the current milestone; [the index](README.md) owns the
sequence. Reproduce the Vulkan contracts through v0.6.0, the
[shared shadows](../design/SHADOW_POLICY.md) included, in `backend/webgpu/`
on Dawn, under the [WebGPU policy](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md), the
[backend boundary](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#4-backends) and
[project layout](../architecture/PROJECT_LAYOUT.md#3-where-new-code-goes).

## Work

- Accept the [WebGPU RFC](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md) before code.
- Stage 1, foundation: the shared backend header in
  [PROJECT_LAYOUT](../architecture/PROJECT_LAYOUT.md) first; `Toon::WebGPU`,
  the Dawn pin and a `webgpu` intent; device, queue and offscreen targets;
  shaders at the root with Tint-validated WGSL; unlit and PreviewSurface
  through `toon-headless --backend webgpu`; settle WGPU-Q1 and WGPU-Q2
  ([policy §11](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#11-open-questions)).
- Stage 2, materials and deformation: per-material bind groups; MToon and
  MMD opaque, transparent and outline pipelines; skinning, morphs and late
  latch; 4x with sample-mask Mask coverage; background modes.
- Stage 3, shadows and presentation: the shared caster pass and receivers;
  `toon-viewport --backend webgpu` with composite, overlay, capture and
  timing.
- Stage 4, acceptance: the [parity scenes](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#9-parity-and-evidence),
  avatar and `shadow-cost` p95/p99 beside Vulkan.
- Cut v0.7.0 through the [release gate](../releases/README.md#release-gate)
  and [release steps](../releases/README.md#how-a-release-is-cut).

## Outside this milestone

Browser/WASM builds, a Hydra or Hgi WebGPU path, GPU texture handoff and
Linux ([policy §12](../design/WEBGPU_POLICY.md#12-outside-this-policy)).
Optimization beyond what the measured p95/p99 asks for.

## Done when

Bounded canonical scenes establish Vulkan/WebGPU semantic and visual parity
for transparent output, outline, Blend, normals, skinning, morphs and
cross-material shadows, with practical performance evidence. Pixel identity
is not required.
