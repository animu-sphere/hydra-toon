# v0.2.0 — MToon quality

v0.1.0 made MToon draw. v0.2.0 makes it draw well enough to use: the MToon
renderer v0.1.0 established, brought to practical quality, rather than new
breadth.

**Done when** a representative VRM avatar displays stably at practical
quality with opaque, mask and blend materials, its textures, skinning,
outline, basic scene lighting and a working anti-aliasing approach, on
Windows and Linux.

## Transparency

MToon's three alpha modes, Opaque, Mask and Blend, each drawn as MToon
means them, so that hair, eyelashes, accessories and semi-transparent
surfaces are right:

- `mtoon_transparent` for Blend, with blending and draw order by alpha mode
  and `renderQueueOffsetNumber`
  ([material policy §6](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#6-transparency-and-draw-order));
- depth writes for transparent materials only with `transparentWithZWrite`;
- alpha test and cutoff for Mask, checked against Blend's ordering;
- double-sidedness, and the outline of a transparent material.

## The rest of MToon

Enough of MToon 1.0 that representative VRM 1.0 avatars draw naturally; not
every feature has to be complete at once:

- UV animation: scroll and rotation by time, masked by `uvAnimationMask`;
- the emissive texture, the shading shift texture and its scale, and the
  normal map, with authored normals read where a mesh has them;
- the texture roles `hdToon` does not read yet, and their texture transforms;
- mask and culling correctness across the avatar's materials.

## Anti-aliasing

Decide the approach and implement it. Silhouettes, outlines, thin geometry,
hair and facial features alias most in a toon renderer. The candidates are
MSAA, TAA, SMAA, FXAA, an outline-specific method, or a combination. If TAA
is part of it, it must resist ghosting under fast motion, pose changes, hair
motion, outlines and facial motion more than a camera-centred renderer's
would.

## Outline stability and cost

The inverted hull is kept and finished before any other method
([design policy §10](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#10-outline)):

- stable width in screen and world units, a world width in metres by the
  stage's `metersPerUnit`;
- aliasing of thin outlines, flicker under animation, and the silhouette's
  temporal stability;
- how the width texture is sampled;
- no hull drawn that cannot be seen, and the pipeline and draw cost
  measured, as a report.

## Scene lights

From the fixed stand-in light to Hydra's lights:

```text
HdLight / scene index → ToonLight → light buffer → MToon
```

Clustered lighting, a production shadow system and complex image-based
lighting are not part of this milestone.

## Linux and more GPU vendors

Vulkan portability beyond Windows and NVIDIA, in this order: Linux with
NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. On Linux x86_64:

- the `hydra` intent built, tested and validated against the Linux leaf of
  the canonical `lookdev` runtime;
- the release workflow building and publishing the Linux package beside the
  Windows one;
- the VRM host session run as a Formation of the Linux runtime, `vrmImaging`'s
  Linux package from `usd-vrm-plugins`' release pins, and the published
  Linux `toon` package.

Each measured configuration is a [report](../reports/) and a row of
[supported configurations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md).
