# v0.4.0 — MMD realization

MMD's material and toon look as a first-class capability, drawn from the
canonical USD stage `usd-mmd-plugins` authors. Parsing PMX, PMD, VMD and VPD,
and MMD's semantics, stay there
([integration scope](../design/INTEGRATION_SCOPE_POLICY.md)). It is built on
the material, morph and animation infrastructure v0.2.0 and v0.3.0 establish
for MToon, not on an MMD-specific architecture.

- **Materials:** MAT-Q4, the MMD edge's width
  ([material policy §9](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#9-open-questions)),
  settled against more captures from MMD (resolved,
  [renderer report 58](../reports/renderer/58-2026-10-12-mat-q4-edge-width.md));
  MMD's self-shadow, which `castSelfShadow` and `receiveSelfShadow` ask for,
  drawn as `full.fx` draws it
  ([material policy §10](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#10-mmds-self-shadow),
  [renderer report 60](../reports/renderer/60-2026-10-12-mmd-self-shadow.md)),
  with MAT-Q5, what its map covers and how far behind it a point is
  shadowed, settled against more captures (resolved,
  [renderer report 66](../reports/renderer/66-2026-10-17-mat-q5-shadow-depth.md));
  its quality raised, with the
  map following the camera, as MMD's own does, and edges that do not step
  and are as soft as MMD's, as the project chose, judged on close-up and
  full-body frames against MMD's from the same cameras and timed in the
  viewport (done,
  [renderer report 63](../reports/renderer/63-2026-10-17-self-shadow-quality.md));
  and MMD's remaining material flags, sharing only what
  [material policy §7](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#7-pipelines-and-parameter-buffers)
  says is common: PMX 2.0's are drawn, but `groundShadow`, which needs a
  floor the stage does not have; PMX 2.1's `vertexColor`, `drawPoints` and
  `drawLines` are read and not drawn, as no local model or MikuMikuDance
  frame has them, and are left for [later](later.md#mmd-after-v040) (done).
- **Morphs:** MMD's vertex and material morphs through v0.3.0's morph and
  parameter paths. A material morph's texture, sphere and toon tints are
  carried
  ([material policy §11](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#11-mmds-material-morphs),
  [renderer report 61](../reports/renderer/61-2026-10-12-mmd-material-morph.md)),
  and MAT-Q6 is settled against MMD's frames of those morphs: MMD draws no
  tint, so they draw only when a host asks for `full.fx`'s reading
  ([renderer report 62](../reports/renderer/62-2026-10-17-mat-q6-morph-tints.md));
  an MMD model's evaluated material morphs draw as MMD draws them, judged
  on MMD's frames of three morphs at two weights (done,
  [renderer report 64](../reports/renderer/64-2026-10-17-mmd-material-morph-evidence.md));
  and its vertex morphs draw as MMD draws them, judged on MMD's frames of
  eight morphs on two models (done,
  [renderer report 65](../reports/renderer/65-2026-10-17-mmd-vertex-morph-evidence.md)).

**Done when** a representative MMD model's look is reproduced at practical
quality from the canonical USD stage.
