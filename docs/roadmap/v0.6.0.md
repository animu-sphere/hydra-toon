# v0.6.0 — Shared shadow and lighting

Unfinished work of the current milestone; [the index](README.md) owns the
sequence. Implement shared scene lights, caster visibility, shadow passes and
resources under [design policy §35](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#35-shared-scene-services)
and the [shadow policy](../design/SHADOW_POLICY.md). Receiver interpretation
belongs to [material policy §14](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#14-shadows-across-material-families).
Share inputs and resources while retaining each material's shading algorithm.

## Work

- Cut v0.6.0 through the [release gate](../releases/README.md#release-gate)
  and [release steps](../releases/README.md#how-a-release-is-cut).

## Outside this milestone

Point-light cube shadows, cascades, advanced soft shadows, ray tracing and
IBL ([shadow policy §10](../design/SHADOW_POLICY.md#10-outside-this-policy)).

## Done when

Different material families cast and receive shared shadows, MToon and MMD
show no representative look regressions, and bounded fixtures record CPU/GPU
timing (including p95/p99) and memory use.
